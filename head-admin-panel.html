Head Administrator SOP & User Manual
Interactive operational guide on managing healthcare staff, resolving alarms & audit procedures
1. How to Register & Verify Doctors & Nurses
Navigate to the Nurse or Doctors section in the sidebar. Click 'Add New Doctor' or 'Add New Nurse' to launch the registration wizard. Fill out council registration numbers, upload credential degrees, verify OTP authentication, and confirm the legal undertaking consent.
2. Handling Emergency SOS Alarms & Code Red
When an SOS alarm triggers, the top notification dot blinks red and sounds an audio chime. Open the Emergency or Alert Mode section to view GPS coordinates and patient vitals. Dispatch the nearest rapid ambulance unit or link the on-call medical specialist.
3. Managing Patient Defaulters & Sanction Enforcement
Go to Defaulters to review patients flagged for repeated no-shows, abusive conduct, or payment defaults. Admin can enforce temporary booking freezes, levy cancellation penalties, or click 'Restore' after dispute resolution.
4. Broadcasting Messages & Push Notifications
Use Message Staff or New Push to compose announcements. Select individual staff members or click 'Select All' to broadcast memos, shift updates, and clinical circulars simultaneously.
